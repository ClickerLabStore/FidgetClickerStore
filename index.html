FAQ

Quick answers before your next click.

What is a tactile clicker fidget?

It is a small handheld fidget built around mechanical-key-style switches. Pressing the keys gives a repeatable tactile response without needing a keyboard.

Which size should I choose?

The 1-key version is the smallest and easiest to carry. Two and three keys add more rhythm and variety, while the 4-key version gives you the largest clicking surface.

Will my cart stay saved?

Yes. This storefront stores cart contents in your browser using localStorage, so refreshing or coming back later on the same browser keeps your cart.

Does checkout take real payments yet?

The static storefront includes the cart and checkout flow. To accept real payments, connect your Stripe, PayPal, Square, or other hosted checkout URL in app.js.
